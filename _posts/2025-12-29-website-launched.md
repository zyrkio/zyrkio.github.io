Great news — **my workshop just received a major upgrade!** ✨  

Over the past weeks, I have been expanding my personal maker and prototyping environment to better support my engineering and product development projects. Three new machines now form the backbone of my fabrication workflow:

- **Makera Z1** — hybrid CNC / laser / milling platform for precision mechanical prototyping  
- **Uniformation GK3 Pro** — high-resolution resin printer for detailed and functional parts  
- **Bambu Lab P2S** — fast and reliable FDM printer for structural components and iteration loops

Together, they allow me to move rapidly from concept to physical prototype and to validate designs across different materials and manufacturing processes.

---

### Why this upgrade matters

My goal is to build a highly flexible engineering workspace to:

- develop **mechanical and electromechanical prototypes**
- iterate concepts using **CNC machining + resin + FDM printing**
- integrate **embedded electronics and NFC-based modules**
- create functional demonstrators for product and system concepts

---

### Capabilities & roles in the workflow

**Makera Z1 (CNC / laser / milling)** enables:

- machining of wood, plastics, and light metals
- fixtures, brackets, and mechanical assemblies
- early structural and kinematic testing

**Uniformation GK3 Pro (resin printing)** is ideal for:

- precise enclosures & sensor housings
- fine mechanical interfaces
- functional prototyping with tight tolerances

**Bambu Lab P2S (FDM printing)** supports:

- fast iteration cycles
- structural parts & functional geometries
- large-format components for assemblies

Each machine covers a distinct prototyping layer — from precision components to structural hardware.

---

### Upcoming projects

Current exploration areas include:

- **CNC-built mechanical systems & mechanisms**
- **Modular NFC reader units for deck boxes**
- electronics & sensor module housings
- custom robotics + UI device components

The combination of digital fabrication, electronics, and software allows me to design **end-to-end prototypes** — from idea to functional system.
